<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/damon-prime4930/1">Damon Prime</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given an integer <strong>n</strong>, determine whether it is a Damon Prime. A number is called a Damon Prime if both <strong>n - 1 </strong>and <strong>n + 1</strong> are prime numbers. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 4<strong>
Output: </strong>true<strong>
Explanation: </strong>4 - 1 = 3 and 4 + 1 = 5. Both 3 and 5 are prime numbers. Hence, 4 is a Damon Prime.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> n = 5<strong>
Output: </strong>false<strong>
Explanation: </strong>5 - 1 = 4 is not a prime number. Hence, 5 is not a Damon Prime.</span></pre></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Prime Number</code>&nbsp;
